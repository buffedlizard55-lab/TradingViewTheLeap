## 4. Limitations that block a "winning" outcome (honest list)

1. **No strategy in this repository produces the required daily compounding.** All three
   pre-registered models are refuted ($0.0 pooled median net profit, 231 windows). The placement
   arithmetic says what the prize costs; nothing here says it can be earned.
2. **No intraday evidence.** Every capture is a daily bar. The contest is decided by realized P/L on
   closed positions, which is an intraday decision process. A daily-bar model is structurally
   handicapped.
3. **Partial universe coverage.** 20 of 94 instruments have verified multipliers/prices/caps and
   only 11 have usable vendor history; 9 selected symbols have a single session of vendor data
   (newly listed contracts). The other 74 eligible futures are transcribed but not modelled.
4. **No authenticated platform validation.** TradingView Strategy Report runs and the Pine
   compiler require a signed-in session this environment does not have, so the Pine file is
   `not_run`.
5. **No trade-level data for anyone.** Champion cards publish only username, net profit, profitable
   share and participant count. No winning strategy can be reverse-engineered from official data.
6. **Point-in-time, correctable board.** Captured frontiers can fall (IR-23), can freeze (H22), and
   hide ranks 251–300 (IR-06).
7. **Vendor-tier price history.** Yahoo is not an exchange; continuous series are roll-unadjusted
   and can create artificial signals.
8. **Eligibility and prize mechanics are personal.** Territory, age, plan status, identity documents
   and payment rails are outside automation.
9. **Real-time data requires a paid or trial plan** (rules §06). Free-plan participants compete with
   delayed data, which materially changes what is possible.
