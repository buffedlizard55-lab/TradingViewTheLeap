## New experiment: target arithmetic

`python3 scripts/target_lab.py` produces five target rows and 100 symbol/target combinations
from existing inputs. This tests required P/L and fixed-exposure price moves, **not profitability**.
At ideal full initial 20:1 exposure, a 5× balance needs +400% account profit and a favorable 20%
underlying move before costs. At 100× it needs +9,900% profit and 495% favorable underlying move.
These are arithmetic equalities, not predictions, achievable-path claims, or ceilings. A short
position cannot generally be assumed to realize such a move; futures price domains and margin
paths require instrument-specific treatment. Compounding is not modeled.
