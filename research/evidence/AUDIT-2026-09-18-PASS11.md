## 2. What is measured vs. what is claimed

* **Measured on committed data:** the exec-summary pending orders (replay of frozen parameters on
  committed vendor bars), the stock-division editions, the gap-fill and latency statistics, the
  benchmark deltas against the Python fill model.
* **Not claimed:** any real TradingView platform result (no authenticated export exists in this
  environment), any real-money outcome, any forecast, and any figure on the site that is not present
  in a committed artifact. The Pine broker-emulator implementation reproduces *documented rules*; it
  is not evidence about how the platform filled a specific historical order.

## 3. Open items carried into the next pass

1. `data/intraday_index.json` is produced by CI (the sandbox has no egress to the vendor). Until it is
   committed, the intraday study and the stock division cannot be derived and the verifier reports
   three deliberate warnings instead of numbers.
2. The authenticated Strategy Report export remains blocked (no credentials, and none are requested);
   the import path is proven only on the labelled synthetic fixture.
3. The 20:1 stocks counterfactual is not an official rule set and must not be read as achievable.
4. The stock division is fitted to one finite vendor window; no out-of-sample season exists yet.
5. Intraday bars are vendor prints with ~60-day (15m) / ~2-year (1h) retention, no bid/ask, no queue
   position and no borrow cost; latency is measured as a price distance, not an executed fill.
