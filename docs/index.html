02Contrarian Strategy Library

Five unique contrarian strategies (C1–C5) designed for maximum explosive paper returns. Each is pre-registered, parameter-frozen, and simulated on real captured vendor prices. These target the rare explosive reversals that the placement arithmetic requires — no risk management, maximum simulated return.

C1 · Contrarian

Capitulation Reversal

Best: 7.24×

A multi-day collapse (or euphoria run) that stretches price by ≥1 ATR over 3 closes with ATR expanding is over-extended and mean-reverts over the next ~10 sessions.

lookback=3, extension_atr_mult=1.0, atr_baseline=50, atr_expansion=1.05, hold_bars=10
Evidence: Best single edition 7.24× (FadeThePanic, E19). 4 editions ≥5× in shadow competition.
Source: competition_results.json
C2 · Contrarian

Gap Fade

Consistent winner

An opening gap larger than 1.5 ATR(14) is over-reaction, not information: fading the gap direction captures the close-the-gap drift over ~5 sessions.

gap_atr_mult=1.5, hold_bars=5
Evidence: GapGoblin most consistent: 7/24 zero-cost editions + latest at 2.87×. Energy and metals show large overnight gaps.
Source: volatility_intelligence.json
C3 · Contrarian

Band-Pierce Reversion

Metals specialist

A close outside the 2-sigma band that re-enters the 1-sigma zone the next session marks the impulse as exhausted; price reverts toward the basis.

band_length=20, band_deviation=2.0, reentry_deviation=1.0, hold_bars=5
Best instruments: COMEX:SI1!, COMEX_MINI:SIL1! (silver), NYMEX:CL1!, NYMEX:NG1!
Source: intel/contrarian.py
C4 · Contrarian

Exhaustion-Bar Reversal

Energy focus

A range-expansion exhaustion bar (true range ≥ 2× ATR with close pinned in extreme quartile) marks liquidation climax; the next sessions revert.

tr_atr_mult=2.0, close_tail_fraction=0.25, hold_bars=5
Best instruments: NYMEX:NG1! (natural gas), NYMEX:CL1!, NYMEX:RB1!
Source: intel/contrarian.py
C5 · Contrarian

Capitulation Pyramider

Season champion

Contrarian capitulation entries followed by full-reinvestment pyramiding every 1 ATR of favourable drift concentrate capital into the rare explosive reversals the placement arithmetic requires.

lookback=3, extension_atr=1.0, add_atr_step=1.0, max_adds=4
Evidence: ContrarianQueen (C5 patient) = season champion (+$2.8M realized). Full 20:1 deployment concentrates into rare winners.

Explosive single-edition outcomes exist; harvesting them is survivorship-bound.

13Method & Verification

The pipeline is deterministic and network-free. Evidence is captured first; the offline verifier checks provenance and re-derives arithmetic.

1

Capture

Save official quotations, exact URLs, and access times

2

Register

Assign source tier and narrowly define what it may prove

3

Derive

Compute return multiples, capacity, thresholds from stored inputs

4

Mutate

Corrupt test copies and prove each verifier check fails

5

Publish

Render pages from checked JSON and fail CI when stale

Reproduce

python3 scripts/verify.py
python3 scripts/verify.py --self-test
python3 scripts/build_intelligence.py
python3 scripts/build_site.py
python3 -m unittest discover -s scripts -p 'test_*.py' -v

Material Limitations

  • The verifier is offline; captured public pages can later change
  • Leaderboard rows and display prices are asynchronous, non-executable snapshots
  • No licensed consistent intraday history covers all eligible symbols
  • Champion summaries contain no trade history — no winning strategy can be reverse-engineered
  • Continuous-contract roll effects require settlement-based series for precision
  • No authenticated TradingView Strategy Report exists for any model
  • Vendor data is front-month continuous with unadjusted roll splices
