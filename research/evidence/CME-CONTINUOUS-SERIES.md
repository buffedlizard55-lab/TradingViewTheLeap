## Series construction

> A continuous futures price series is a seamless price series representing either the price of the Active Contract ... or the Front Contract.
> The daily values are based on the official settlement prices of the underlying futures, and published by CME Group shortly after the settlement prices are released.

## Backtesting use and access

> Design and validate trading strategies using extensive historical data across 36 underlying products.
> Use the Total Volume and Open Interest (OI) data to gauge realistic market capacity.
> Historical and daily Volume/Open Interest files can be acquired from DataMine.

This is an official option for licensed, settlement-based futures research. The repository does not
contain or claim access to the licensed dataset.
