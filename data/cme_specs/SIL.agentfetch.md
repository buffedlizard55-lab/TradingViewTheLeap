## Micro Silver Futures - Contract Specs

| Contract Unit | 1,000 troy ounces |
| Price Quotation | U.S. dollars and cents per troy ounce |
| Trading Hours | CME Globex:<br>Sunday 5:00 p.m. - Friday - 4:00 p.m. CT with a 60-minute break each day beginning at 4:00 p.m. CT<br>TAS: Sunday - Friday 5:00 p.m. - 12:25 p.m. CT<br>CME ClearPort:<br>Sunday 5:00 p.m. - Friday 4:00 p.m. CT with no reporting Monday - Thursday from 4:00 p.m. - 5:00 p.m. CT |
| Minimum Price Fluctuation | Outright:<br>0.005 per troy ounce = $5.00<br>Settlement: 0.001 per troy ounce = $1.00<br>TAS: Zero or +/- 10 ticks in the minimum tick increment of the outright<br>CALENDAR SPREAD<br>0.001 per troy ounce = $1.00 |
| Product Code | CME Globex: SIL<br>CME ClearPort: SIL<br>Clearing: SIL<br>TAS: MST |
| Listed Contracts | Monthly contracts listed for 3 consecutive months and any Jan, Mar, May, July, Sep and Dec within the nearest 12 months. |
| Settlement Method | Deliverable |
| Termination of Trading | Trading terminates on the third last business day of the contract month. |
| TAM or TAS Rules | Trading at Settlement (TAS) is subject to the requirements of Rule 524.A. TAS trades off a "Base Price" of zero (equal to the daily settlement price) to create a differential versus the daily settlement price in the underlying futures contract month. The TAS clearing price equals the daily settlement price of the underlying futures contract month plus or minus the TAS transaction price.<br>[TAS Table](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/files/tas-tam-eligibility.xlsx) |
