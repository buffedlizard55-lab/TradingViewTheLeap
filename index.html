Ranking mechanics

Place is determined by realized profit/loss on closed positions. The leaderboard updates no more than once per hour. Open positions are automatically closed at the competition end and included in the final calculation. Closing changes when P/L becomes realized; it does not manufacture profit.

Prize qualification also requires activity on at least 5 UTC days. The official definition: At least one action that results in opening or closing a position between 00:00:00 and 23:59:59 UTC.

Strategy candidates and test tools

S1refuted

Donchian trend breakout

A close beyond the prior channel, filtered by moving-average direction and ATR expansion, captures persistent one-way moves better than an unfiltered breakout.

Entry
Long above the prior Donchian high when the fast EMA is above the slow EMA and ATR exceeds its baseline; short below the prior Donchian low under the opposite trend condition.
Exit
Opposite signal, optional ATR bracket, or the configured test-window end.
Reject when
Reject if walk-forward median net profit is not positive, if the lower confidence bound is not positive, or if results depend on one symbol or one window.
S2refuted

EMA impulse continuation

A fast/slow EMA crossover accompanied by above-baseline ATR identifies momentum transitions that persist long enough to overcome modeled costs.

Entry
Long on a fast EMA cross above the slow EMA during ATR expansion; short on the inverse cross.
Exit
Opposite signal, optional ATR bracket, or the configured test-window end.
Reject when
Reject if out-of-sample net profit is non-positive after costs or if performance reverses under nearby EMA lengths.
S3refuted

Bollinger squeeze release

A close outside the Bollinger envelope after bandwidth compression captures volatility expansion without predicting whether the break will be up or down.

Entry
Long above the upper band or short below the lower band when prior bandwidth was near its rolling minimum and current bandwidth expands.
Exit
Opposite signal, optional ATR bracket, or the configured test-window end.
Reject when
Reject if out-of-sample profit is non-positive, trade count is too small for the declared confidence interval, or small parameter changes erase the result.

Sensitivity grid and declared assumptions
  • Signals on bar close fill at the next bar's open (Pine broker-emulator default).
  • End-of-window open positions close at the final bar's close (contest auto-close analog).
  • Whole contracts; position size min(rules cap, buying power); no pyramiding.
  • No margin-call liquidation simulated; margin breaches are counted, not acted on.
  • Slippage is a declared fraction of signal-bar ATR(14) on both legs; commissions per side.
  • Vendor series are front-month continuous futures with unadjusted rolls; roll gaps can create artificial signals.

Method, verification, and limits

The site is deterministic and network-free. Public evidence is captured first; the offline verifier then checks provenance and re-derives arithmetic.

1Capture

Save official quotations, exact URLs, and access times.

2Register

Assign source tier and narrowly define what it may prove.

3Derive

Compute return multiples, capacity, thresholds, and counts from stored inputs.

4Mutate

Corrupt test copies and prove each critical verifier check fails.

5Publish

Render this page from checked JSON and fail CI when it is stale.

Reproduce

python3 scripts/verify.py
python3 scripts/verify.py --self-test
python3 scripts/build_site.py
python3 -m py_compile scripts/verify.py scripts/build_site.py

Verifier coverage

  • Rule constants, timestamps, prize continuity, cash arithmetic, and payment methods.
  • Leaderboard percentage/dollar consistency and live-record synchronization.
  • Quote provenance and all capacity fields.
  • Universe caps, contract multipliers, and CSV parity.
  • Both exact stock endpoint windows per record and every adjusted-close ratio.
  • Strategy files, contest settings, and explicit untested/null result state.

Material limitations

  • The verifier is offline; captured public pages can later change.
  • Leaderboard rows and display prices are asynchronous, non-executable snapshots.
  • No licensed consistent intraday history covers all eligible symbols here.
  • Champion summaries contain no trade history.
  • Continuous-contract roll effects require separate testing.
  • No authenticated Strategy Report or forward-test artifact exists.
Scope. This project concerns a virtual-money competition. It is not investment advice, a return forecast, or evidence that any historical result will repeat. TradingView and CME Group are not affiliated with this repository.
