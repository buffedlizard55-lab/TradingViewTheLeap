## SOL Futures - Contract Specs

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| Contract Unit | 500 SOL, as defined by the CME CF Solana-Dollar Reference Rate (SOLUSD\_RR) |
| Price Quotation | U.S. dollars and cents per SOL |
| Trading Hours | CME Globex:<br>24/7 with the exception of the following maintenance windows: Saturday 2:00 a.m. to 4:00 a.m. CT & Monday-Friday 4:00 p.m. to 4:02 p.m. CT<br>BTIC London: 24/7 with the exception of the following maintenance windows: Saturday: 2:00 a.m. to 4:00 a.m. CT & Monday-Friday from 10:00 a.m. to 10:05 a.m. CT<br>BTIC NY: 24/7 with the exception of the following maintenance windows: Saturday: 2:00 a.m. to 4:00 a.m. CT & Monday-Friday from 3:00 p.m. to 3:05 p.m. CT<br>TAS: 24/7 with the exception of the following maintenance windows: Saturday 2:00 a.m. to 4:00 a.m. CT & Monday-Friday from 3:00 p.m. to 3:05 p.m. CT<br>CME ClearPort:<br>24/7 with the exception of the maintenance window on Saturday from 2:00 a.m. - 4:00 a.m. CT.<br>BTIC London/NY: 24/7 with the exception of the following maintenance window on Saturday: 2:00 a.m. to 4:00 a.m. CT. Trades for the following day’s Reference Rate can be submitted after 4:00 p.m. CT. Submissions after 4:00 p.m. CT Friday will price to Monday's Reference Rate.<br>TAS: 24/7 with the exception of the maintenance window on Saturday from 2:00 a.m. - 4:00 a.m. CT. |
| Minimum Price Fluctuation | $0.05 per SOL = $25.00 per contract<br>BTIC: $0.01 per SOL = $5.00 per contract<br>TAS: Zero or +/- 20 ticks at $0.01 per SOL = $5.00<br>Calendar Spread: $0.01 per SOL = $5.00 per contract |
| Product Code | CME Globex: SOL<br>CME ClearPort: SOL<br>Clearing: SOL<br>TAS: TSL<br>BTIC: "SLB","SNB" |
| Listed Contracts | Monthly contracts listed for 6 consecutive months, quarterly contracts (Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec) listed for 4 additional quarters and a second Dec contract if only one is listed. |
| Settlement Method | Financially Settled |
| Floating Price |  |
| Termination of Trading | Trading terminates at 4:00 p.m. London time on the last Friday of the contract month. If this is not both a London and U.S. business day, trading terminates on the prior London or U.S. business day.<br>BTIC London: Trading terminates at 4:00 p.m. London time on the business day immediately preceding the day of Final Settlement Price determination for such futures contract. For clarity, BTIC transactions in expiring futures contracts may not be initiated on the Last Trade Date in such expiring futures.<br>BTIC NY: Trading terminates at 4:00 p.m. New York time on the business day immediately preceding the day of Final Settlement Price determination for such futures contract. For clarity, BTIC transactions in expiring futures contracts may not be initiated on the Last Trade Date in such expiring futures.<br>TAS: Trading terminates at 3:00 p.m. CT on the U.S. business day immediately preceding the last trade date for such futures contract. TAS transactions in expiring futures contracts may not be initiated on the Last Trade Date in such expiring futures. |

| TAM or TAS Rules | Trading at Settlement (TAS) is subject to the requirements of Rule 524.A. TAS trades off a "Base Price" of zero (equal to the daily settlement price) to create a differential versus the daily settlement price in the underlying futures contract month. The TAS clearing price equals the daily settlement price of the underlying futures contract month plus or minus the TAS transaction price. TAS available in the nearest three months.<br>[TAS Table](https://www.cmegroup.com/trading/files/tas-tam-eligibility.xlsx) |
